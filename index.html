⚠️ The Golden Rules — how to train safely

Before you touch a live account, tattoo these three rules onto your process. Every mentor worth paying for will tell you the same thing.

1

Demo first, always

Trade a demo account on real-time price feeds for 3–6 months before risking a single rupee or dollar. Prove your edge is repeatable before it's expensive to be wrong.

2

Watch the macro, not just the chart

Gold moves inverse to the US Dollar (DXY) and reacts hard to CPI, NFP, and interest rate decisions. Half a professional's week is spent reading macroeconomics, not candlesticks.

3

Risk 1–2%, never more

Gold and most instruments are notoriously volatile. Never risk more than 1–2% of your total account balance on a single trade structure — no exceptions, no "just this once."
